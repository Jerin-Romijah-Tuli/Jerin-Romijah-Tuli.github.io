About

Objective

Seeking a fully funded MS/PhD position in artificial intelligence and machine learning research, with a focus on natural language processing, LLM evaluation, and deep learning for biomedical signals. My work centers on making model outputs trustworthy under uncertainty — teaching systems to recognize what they don't know, whether that's an unanswerable question in a retrieval-augmented pipeline or an ambiguous EEG epoch at a sleep-stage boundary.

The Social Mirage

Studies whether instruction-tuned LLMs keep their own internal beliefs private while facing social pressure to conform, using representation-level probing.

A spectral-enhancement module paired with per-channel vision transformers and cross-channel fusion for automated sleep-stage classification from EEG/EOG signals.
