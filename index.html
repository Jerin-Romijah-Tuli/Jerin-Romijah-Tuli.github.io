02Research projects

Automated EEG Sleep Stage Classification

undergraduate thesis · targeting IEEE JBHI

A deterministic spectral-enhancement module feeding per-channel vision transformers with gated cross-channel fusion, for 5-stage sleep classification from EEG/EOG. Trained on the full Sleep-EDF Expanded corpus (153 cassette + 44 telemetry recordings, ~51GB) with a rebuilt, verified subject-independent split.

Macro-F1 78.77%   Accuracy 78.91%   κ 0.734   Params 46.3M

Bengali RAG Hallucination Evaluation Pipeline

independent research · targeting MeLLM workshop, ACL 2026

A three-condition evaluation design isolating retrieval failure from generation failure in Bengali retrieval-augmented generation, built on csebuetnlp/squad_bn with Groq-hosted LLMs. Introduces a Language Gap Score benchmarking Bengali RAG performance against an English baseline.

BanglishBench

accepted, SemEval 2026

A 1,130-sample Bengali–English code-switching benchmark spanning safety classification, NLI, and QA, with a composite BBS metric combining MCC, Cohen's κ, G-Mean, FNR, and CSI.
